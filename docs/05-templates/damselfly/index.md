# Damselfly

1-Click installation template for Damselfly on Easypanel

## Description

Damselfly is a modern, self-hosted photo management system that helps you organize, search, and share your photo collection. It uses AI-powered image recognition to automatically tag and categorize your photos, making it easy to find specific images in your collection.

## Instructions

Add your photos and libraries in the respected volumes or change them to bind volumes as per your need.

## Benefits

- AI-Powered Organization: Automatically tag and categorize photos using machine learning
- Fast Search: Quickly find photos using tags, metadata, or visual search
- Privacy-Focused: Keep your photos private with self-hosted storage
- Modern Interface: Clean, responsive web interface for managing your photos

## Features

- Automatic Tagging: AI-powered image recognition for automatic photo tagging
- Face Recognition: Identify and group photos by people in them
- Metadata Extraction: Extract and search by EXIF data and image metadata
- Duplicate Detection: Find and manage duplicate photos in your collection
- Thumbnail Generation: Automatic thumbnail generation for fast browsing
- Search & Filter: Advanced search and filtering capabilities
- Export Options: Export photos with metadata and tags
